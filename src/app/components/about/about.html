Rules of the Realm

Command warriors, wizards, monsters, and legions from the council table. These rules mirror how the realm resolves heroes, weapons, monsters, battles, and chronicles.

Heroes & Gear

  • Hero names are required, must be unique, and can be no longer than 20 characters.
  • Every hero begins with 100 HP.
  • A hero can carry one equipped weapon and one equipped armor piece.
  • A hero without a weapon deals 0 damage in battle.

Weapons

  • Weapon names can be no longer than 20 characters.
  • Weapon damage must be greater than 0.
  • A hero can equip one weapon at a time; choosing another weapon replaces the current one.

Monsters

  • Monsters begin with 100 HP by default.
  • Monster damage must be between 0 and 100.
  • Monster defense reduces incoming hero damage by a percentage.

Combat

  • Each round, the monster strikes first, then the hero retaliates.
  • Battle continues until either the hero or the monster reaches 0 HP.
  • Health is clamped between 0 and 100 after every hit.
  • No damage is exchanged if both fighters are already at 0 HP, both sides deal 0 damage, or both defenses fully block damage.

Hero hit: weapon damage reduced by the monster's defense percentage.

Monster hit: monster damage reduced by the hero armor's defense percentage.

Legions

  • Legion names are required, must be unique, and can be no longer than 40 characters.
  • A legion can hold up to 3 heroes.
  • Only living heroes can join a legion.
  • A hero must have a weapon with damage greater than 0 to join.
  • A hero cannot be added twice to the same legion.

Battle Chronicles

Every completed battle is preserved in the realm's records with the winner, loser, remaining HP, and fighter IDs. Visit Battle Logs to read the latest chronicles.
